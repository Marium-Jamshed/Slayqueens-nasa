How to play

  1. Pick a base. Moon or Mars. Then launch.
  2. Split 8 crew hours. Drag the sliders for Power, Air, Food, Shield and Science.
  3. Watch the rings. Blue is healthy. Red is danger.
  4. Press End day. Your plan changes the base.
  5. Make story choices. Your crew will ask you to decide. Every choice has a cost.
  6. Survive 15 days. Science earns points. The mission ends early if Power, Air or Food hits zero, or Radiation hits 100.

Keys: 1 to 5 add an hour. Shift + 1 to 5 remove one. E ends the day.
